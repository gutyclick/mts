# Support conversations implementation plan

**Goal:** Give SIM a complete conversation lifecycle: choose an issue, resume history, finish manually or after inactivity, rate support and start a separate issue.

**Architecture:** Extend existing Supabase support threads rather than install another chat service. Server-owned conversation state and an opaque guest cookie protect history and feedback. Keep human tickets open until explicitly resolved. The widget remains loaded on demand.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16, React 19, Supabase/PostgreSQL, existing support API, Lucide and MyTripSim design tokens.

## Product decisions

- Close AI chats after 10 minutes without messages, as explicitly confirmed by the owner; one server-side setting controls the deadline.
- Closing the support conversation does not sign the customer out of their account. The X minimizes the widget; resolving an issue is a separate labelled action.
- Feedback is optional, one 1–5 score per closed conversation, plus an optional redacted comment. Starting a new issue never depends on leaving a score.
- Authenticated and guest history stay isolated. A seven-day HttpOnly cookie identifies guests; no conversations, QR codes or credentials are placed in browser storage.
- Guest chats are persisted to support resume/feedback, explained in the privacy copy, and removed after 30 days of inactivity. Tickets requiring account/order access keep the existing authentication requirements.
- New issues start with a new thread, empty composer and no inherited order context. Closed issues are read-only. Human replies continue within their open ticket.

## Implementation

1. Extend thread ownership, issue topics, closure reason/time and rating fields. Add server-only RPCs for open, close, feedback and chat turns, with ownership checks, idempotency and a database expiry job. Test closure/AI races, environment-independent ownership and guest/account isolation in PGlite.
2. Add protected conversation endpoints, guest-cookie setup, authoritative deadlines and history. Keep AI budget limits and the low-cost model. Update chat transport to persist guest turns and reject writes to closed threads.
3. Add the messenger home/history view, topic choices and conversation controls. Reuse the existing chat UI, ticket escalation and messages. Add an accessible optional feedback card and server-driven inactivity handling.
4. Display ratings in the customer ticket detail and staff view. Prevent resolved issues from silently reopening; offer a new issue instead. Update privacy information for guest conversation storage.
5. Run focused unit/security tests, desktop/mobile ES/EN lifecycle tests, build, lint and secret scanning. Apply the migration, deploy and verify read-only production entry points. Do not send test notifications to staff or create paid/provider orders.

Execution uses the executing-plans workflow within the owner's requested implementation scope; no agent delegation is needed.
